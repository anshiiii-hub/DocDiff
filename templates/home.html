Why DocDiff

Everything you need to review with confidence

Whether you're approving a contract, grading a resubmission, or auditing a policy — see exactly what changed, not just that something did.

Spot what's new

New paragraphs and clauses stand out instantly — so you can focus on fresh content instead of re-reading everything.

Catch what disappeared

Removed text is clearly struck through. Nothing slips through when clauses or requirements get quietly dropped.

Pinpoint every edit

Small wording tweaks? Word-level highlighting shows the exact characters that changed — not just the whole paragraph.
